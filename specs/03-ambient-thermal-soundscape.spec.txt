Title: Ambient Thermal Soundscape
Status: Draft
Priority: Near-term 3

Goal
Improve immersion with subtle bubbling/hissing sounds around hot and boiling pools.

Current State
- Visual steam exists.
- No local ambient audio behavior.

Scope
- Add low-volume ambient loops or periodic one-shots near hot spring water.
- Different intensity for hot vs boiling water.
- Allow server-side disable or volume tuning.

Out of Scope
- Complex dynamic music system.
- Voice/UI audio features.

Design
1. Choose sound assets:
   - hot_springs_bubble.ogg
   - hot_springs_hiss.ogg
2. Trigger strategy:
   - Preferred: periodic one-shot sounds near players in range.
   - Avoid always-on per-node loops that can stack too much.
3. Range model:
   - Scan small radius around player for hot/boiling nodes.
   - If present, play occasional sound with randomized pitch/gain.
4. Settings:
   - hot_springs_ambient_sound_enabled
   - hot_springs_ambient_sound_interval
   - hot_springs_ambient_sound_gain

Implementation Steps
1. Add sound files under textures/sounds location used by mod.
2. Add per-player ambient timer state.
3. Implement periodic area check and probabilistic sound play.
4. Add separate probabilities/intensity for boiling node proximity.
5. Add settings and defaults.

Testing Plan
- Approach pool: hear subtle ambient audio.
- Leave pool area: ambient sound stops naturally.
- Boiling pools: stronger/more frequent cues than normal hot pools.
- Disable setting: no ambient sounds play.

Acceptance Criteria
- Nearby pools feel sonically alive without being noisy.
- No rapid stacking or clipping from overlapping sounds.
- Server admin can tune or disable ambient audio.

Risks and Mitigations
- Risk: too many overlapping sounds.
  Mitigation: per-player timer with min interval and random gating.
- Risk: missing assets break playback.
  Mitigation: include asset existence checks and fallback behavior.

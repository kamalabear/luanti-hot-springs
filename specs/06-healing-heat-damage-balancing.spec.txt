Title: Healing and Heat Damage Balancing
Status: Draft
Priority: Near-term 6

Goal
Implement a clear risk/reward loop: moderate pools heal slowly, extreme pools harm players.

Current State
- Boiling water source currently applies direct damage_per_second.
- Healing behavior is not implemented.

Scope
- Add healing over time for safe temperature classes.
- Add or refine damage over time for dangerous classes.
- Balance rates and intervals using configurable settings.

Out of Scope
- Complex RPG buffs/debuff trees.
- PvP-specific balancing rules.

Design
1. Use variable temperature API as source of truth.
2. Globalstep tick applies effects to players in hot spring water:
   - warm: small heal over time
   - hot: neutral or tiny heal (configurable)
   - scalding: damage over time
3. Use per-player timer accumulator to avoid per-frame hp changes.
4. Add settings:
   - heal_per_tick
   - damage_per_tick
   - effect_tick_seconds
   - max_heal_temperature_class

Implementation Steps
1. Add player effect manager table with elapsed timers.
2. Integrate node/temperature detection for current player position.
3. Apply hp change at configured intervals with bounds checks.
4. Reconcile with node-level damage_per_second to avoid double damage.
5. Add optional chat debug logs behind a debug setting.

Testing Plan
- Warm pools heal at expected rate.
- Scalding pools damage at expected rate.
- Moving between pool classes updates behavior quickly.
- No effect applied when player is outside hot spring water.

Acceptance Criteria
- Healing and damage behavior is predictable and tunable.
- No accidental double-application of damage.
- Multiplayer behavior is consistent per player.

Risks and Mitigations
- Risk: node damage and script damage stack unexpectedly.
  Mitigation: choose one source of damage and document it clearly.
- Risk: balance feels unfair.
  Mitigation: expose rates as settings and iterate from playtests.

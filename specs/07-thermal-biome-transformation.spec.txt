Title: Thermal Biome Transformation
Status: Implemented
Priority: Medium-term 1

Goal
Make hot springs feel alive by having their heat visibly affect the surrounding environment — melting snow in cold biomes and occasionally spawning moss near the pool edge.

Current State
- Hot spring water nodes have temperature data but no environmental interaction.
- Snow, ice, and frozen terrain remain unchanged when a hot spring generates nearby.

Scope
- Melt snow and ice on solid blocks adjacent to warm or hotter water.
- Occasionally convert exposed stone/dirt near pool edges to mossy variants.
- Tie transformation intensity to water temperature (warmer water = larger radius).

Out of Scope
- Permanent biome alteration or mapblock rewriting.
- Plant growth or tree spawning.
- Player-placed block protection (operates on worldgen-generated nodes only).

Design
1. Melting:
   - When a warm/hot/scalding water node is placed or generated, scan adjacent blocks.
   - Replace `default:snow`, `default:snowblock`, `default:ice` with the underlying bare block (e.g., `default:dirt_with_snow` → `default:dirt`, `default:ice` → `default:water_source`).
   - Radius scales with temperature class: warm = 1 node, hot = 2 nodes, scalding = 3 nodes.
2. Moss growth:
    - Low-probability conversion (25%) of exposed stone or dirt adjacent to non-scalding hot springs water to `default:mossycobble` or `default:dirt_with_grass`.
   - Only fires during worldgen (on_generated), not during gameplay.
   - Scalding water does not grow moss (too hot).
3. Both effects are purely worldgen-time, not runtime ABMs.

Implementation Steps
1. Add melting scan to CID-8 on_generated callback (or a new CID).
2. Add moss growth scan with probability gate.
3. Use temperature class of pool water to determine radius.
4. Ensure no player-placed node is touched (check node owner or only modify default:* nodes).

Testing Plan
- Place warm water next to snow in a cold biome → snow is replaced.
- Place scalding water next to ice → ice melts within 3-node radius.
- Moss grows on stone adjacent to warm water at expected probability.
- No changes occur outside the configured radius.

Acceptance Criteria
- Cold-biome hot springs visibly melt surrounding snow and ice.
- Moss patches appear organically near warm pool edges.
- Both effects run only during worldgen with no runtime cost.

Risks and Mitigations
- Risk: melting creates unexpected water sources.
  Mitigation: only melt snow/ice on solid blocks, not water-adjacent nodes.
- Risk: moss growth looks unnatural if too dense.
  Mitigation: low probability and only on natural stone/dirt nodes.
